why we're here

about

up on the clouds started with a simple idea:
what if the internet had a little more room for joy?

we make tiny games and happy little moments
designed to give you something positive to come back to.

but there's something bigger behind it, too.

we're working toward supporting teen mental health
and creating a space where young people can
take a breath, reset, and remember that they're not alone.
